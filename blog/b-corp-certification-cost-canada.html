What B Corp Certification Costs in Canada.

Most businesses that ask about B Corp certification cost get a partial answer. B Lab's fee schedule is public. What you actually spend to reach 80 points usually isn't discussed.

What B Lab charges

B Lab's annual certification fee is revenue-based and charged in US dollars. Companies under $1M in annual revenue pay US$1,000 per year. Between $1M and $5M: US$2,000. Between $5M and $10M: US$3,000. The fee scales upward from there.

These are recurring fees. You pay them every year to maintain your certification status. Most first-time applicants don't fully account for this when planning their budget.

There's also a verification fee, separate from the annual certification fee, charged when B Lab reviews your submission. For small businesses, this runs approximately US$1,500 to US$3,500 depending on company size and complexity. Budget for both.

What assessment preparation actually costs

The B Impact Assessment itself is free to access. Anyone can create a profile at bimpactassessment.net and begin scoring their business today. B Lab does not charge to use the tool or complete the initial assessment.

The cost comes from closing the gaps between your current score and the 80-point threshold required for certification. Policy documents need to exist. Governance structures need to be formalized. Supply chain data has to be compiled before the third-party verification call. This is where most businesses either stall or hire outside help.

Working with a B Corp consultant in Canada typically costs $3,000 to $6,000 CAD for a small business, depending on how close the business already is to 80 points. Businesses that come to the process with documented HR practices, an existing governance structure, and accessible supplier data spend less. Businesses starting from zero on documentation spend more.

"The gap is documentation, not an overhaul. Most businesses are closer to 80 points than they think."

What this costs in time

A well-organized business — HR policies drafted, governance documents in place, supplier data accessible — completes preparation in six to twelve weeks. Add B Lab's verification queue and you're looking at four to six months total. Starting from zero commonly adds three to six months of gap-closing work first. The B Corp timeline depends almost entirely on how organized the business is going in.

Budget note

B Lab fees are quoted in USD. For Canadian businesses, the exchange rate adds roughly 35–40% to the USD figure at current rates. A US$2,000 annual fee is closer to $2,700–$2,800 CAD. Account for this in your planning.
