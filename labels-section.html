08 — What these labels mean

Three certifications.
All different things.

B Corp, Climate Neutral, and 1% for the Planet are real and worthwhile — but they measure different things and ask different things of your business.

01
B Corp Your whole business. Independently checked.

A third party — B Lab — assesses how your business treats its people, community, and environment across five areas. It's the most rigorous of the three. Scored, verified, and renewed every three years. The logo people actually recognise.

Climate Neutral Your carbon footprint. Measured and offset.

Focused only on emissions. You measure your footprint, commit to a reduction plan, and offset what remains. More specific than B Corp — no workers or governance scoring — and renewed annually. Good for businesses where carbon is the primary story.

03
1% for the Planet One percent of revenue. Donated every year.

No audit. A public commitment to donate 1% of annual revenue to approved environmental nonprofits. The credibility comes from the network and the public accountability of the commitment — not from third-party verification of your operations.

B Corp ≠ Benefit Corporation — not the same thing
B Corp
A certification.
Issued by B Lab.

Earned through a scored assessment. Any company structure can apply. The logo you see on products and storefronts. What most businesses actually want.

Benefit Corporation
A legal structure.
Registered with the government.

Changes what your board can legally prioritise beyond shareholder profit. Not available in all provinces. You can have one without the other — and most businesses do.

The two get confused constantly. If someone has quoted you on "Benefit Corporation registration" when you wanted the B Corp logo, that's worth a conversation before you proceed.
