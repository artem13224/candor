What you can expect from Candor, what Candor expects from you, and how fees, cancellation, and refunds work — in plain language.

Effective October 4, 2026 · Last updated October 4, 2026

Candor ("Candor," "we," "us," or "our") is operated by Artem Furman, Vancouver, British Columbia, Canada. These terms apply to candorcertified.com (the "Site"), the free tools on it, and — unless a signed engagement agreement says otherwise — the consulting services we provide.

By using the Site or engaging Candor, you agree to these terms. If you're using the Site on behalf of a business, you confirm you're authorised to accept them for that business.

1. What Candor does

Candor provides independent advisory services that help small businesses prepare for sustainability certifications such as B Corp, Climate Neutral, and 1% for the Planet. We assess, advise, draft, and organise. We do not grant certifications, and we are not affiliated with, endorsed by, or part of B Lab, Climate Neutral, 1% for the Planet, or any other certifying body.

Those bodies make their own decisions under their own standards and timelines, which change. Candor cannot and does not guarantee that your business will be certified, that it will achieve any particular score, or that a certifying body will respond within any particular time. Timelines and scores quoted on the Site or in an audit are good-faith estimates based on the information you give us.

2. Engagements and scope

Paid work starts with a short written engagement agreement (usually an email summary you confirm) that states the tier, scope, deliverables, schedule, and fee. If anything in that agreement conflicts with these terms, the engagement agreement wins for that engagement.

The three standard tiers and their published prices are described on the Services section of the Site. Work outside the agreed scope — additional entities, a second certification, a further submission round — is quoted in writing before it starts and only proceeds if you approve it.

3. Fees, payment, and taxes

  • All fees are quoted and invoiced in Canadian dollars and exclude applicable taxes (GST and, where applicable, PST), which are added to invoices as required by law.
  • Fees charged by certifying bodies — for example B Lab's certification and annual fees — are separate, are paid by you directly to that body, and are never collected or refunded by Candor.
  • Unless your engagement agreement says otherwise: the Readiness Audit is payable in full before the kickoff call; the Gap-Closing Engagement and Full Path to Certification are billed 50% on signing and the balance in milestones set out in the engagement agreement.
  • Invoices are due within 14 days. We may pause work on overdue accounts after giving you written notice.

4. Cancellation and refunds

We'd rather you leave early than stay unhappy, so the rules are simple:

SituationWhat happens

Readiness Audit — cancelled after kickoff We stop work, deliver whatever has been completed, and refund the unearned portion of the fee on a pro-rata basis. Once the written report has been delivered, the audit fee is non-refundable.

14-business-day delivery promise For the first new engagement Candor takes on in each calendar quarter, the Readiness Audit report is delivered within 14 business days of receiving your completed documents. If Candor misses that date for reasons on its side, the audit fee is waived. Delays caused by missing documents pause the clock.
Certification not achieved Our fees pay for the advisory work, not the outcome. They are not refundable because a certifying body declines, delays, or scores an application differently than estimated. The Full Path tier includes one revision round for exactly this situation.

To cancel, email candorcertified@gmail.com. Refunds go back to the original payment method.

5. What we need from you

Certification work depends on accurate information. You agree to give us complete and truthful information about your business, to respond to document requests within a reasonable time, and to review drafts before they are submitted anywhere. Delays on your side push the schedule; they don't change the fee. We will never knowingly help a business misrepresent its practices to a certifying body, and we may end the engagement if asked to.

6. Not legal, tax, or financial advice

Candor advises on certification readiness. Our policy drafts, gap analyses, and recommendations are not legal, accounting, or tax advice, and nothing on the Site is. Legal structure changes (such as adopting benefit-corporation provisions), tax treatment, and regulatory compliance claims should be reviewed by a qualified professional before you rely on them.

7. Confidentiality

We treat everything you share during an engagement as confidential, use it only to do the work, and don't disclose it except to service providers who need it (see our Privacy Policy), to a certifying body at your direction, or where the law requires. We may describe the engagement in general terms (industry, tier, outcome) in our own marketing only with your written permission.

8. Ownership of the work

Once an engagement is paid in full, you own the deliverables created for you — reports, policies, evidence packages, and launch materials. Candor keeps ownership of its underlying templates, frameworks, and know-how, and may reuse them for other clients in a way that never reveals your confidential information.

9. Free tools and content

The self-assessment tool, blog, and other free content are provided for general information. Assessment results are modelled estimates derived from a handful of answers — they are not a B Impact Assessment score, a certification decision, or a promise about your timeline. Articles reflect our understanding at the time of writing; certification standards change, so check the certifying body's current requirements before acting.
